# AML and Regulatory Reporting (sample)
> **Illustrative sample policy.** Written for the SentinelAI demo. It is NOT an official RBI, FIU-IND or NPCI document and must not be relied on for compliance. Replace this folder with your institution's approved policies; the retrieval engine indexes whatever Markdown files are placed here.

## AML-1 Suspicious transaction reporting
AML-1.1 When the principal officer concludes a transaction is suspicious, a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) is furnished to FIU-IND within 7 days of arriving at that conclusion.
AML-1.2 The customer must not be told that an STR has been or will be filed (no tipping-off).
AML-1.3 Confirmed mule-account cases are always assessed for STR filing.

## AML-2 Cash and threshold reporting
AML-2.1 Cash Transaction Reports (CTR) cover cash transactions above Rs 10 lakh, or integrally connected cash transactions exceeding that amount within a month, and are filed monthly.
AML-2.2 Splitting transactions to stay below reporting thresholds (structuring) is itself a red flag and must be escalated.

## AML-3 Record keeping
AML-3.1 Transaction records and KYC records are retained for at least 5 years after the business relationship ends.
AML-3.2 The fraud audit log (scores, reasons, analyst actions) is retained for the same period.
