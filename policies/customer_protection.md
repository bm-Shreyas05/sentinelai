# Customer Protection and Dispute Handling (sample)
> **Illustrative sample policy.** Written for the SentinelAI demo. It is NOT an official RBI, FIU-IND or NPCI document and must not be relied on for compliance. Replace this folder with your institution's approved policies; the retrieval engine indexes whatever Markdown files are placed here.

## CP-1 Customer notification
CP-1.1 Customers are notified of blocked transactions immediately by SMS or app notification.
CP-1.2 Notifications must include a way to confirm or deny the transaction and a reminder never to share OTP, PIN or card details.

## CP-2 Unauthorised transaction reporting
CP-2.1 Customers who report an unauthorised electronic transaction promptly (within 3 working days) have zero liability where the fault lies with the bank or a third party.
CP-2.2 Delayed reporting may increase customer liability according to the bank's board-approved policy.
CP-2.3 Disputed amounts are shadow-credited to the customer within 10 working days of the report while the investigation continues.

## CP-3 False positives
CP-3.1 When an analyst marks a blocked transaction as a false positive, the hold is released and the customer is informed within 1 hour.
CP-3.2 False-positive rates are tracked weekly; a rate above 8% of alerts triggers a threshold review.
